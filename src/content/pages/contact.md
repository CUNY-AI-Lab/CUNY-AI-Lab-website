The CUNY AI Lab welcomes collaboration from faculty, researchers, and students across CUNY who are interested in exploring ethical AI experimentation for teaching and research purposes. While the Lab is in its pilot phase, access is open to everyone at the Graduate Center and to selected faculty and staff from other campuses; students elsewhere at CUNY join through a pilot class or project.
