Map and Notebook

Use the footer to switch between Map and Notebook. Place items at Micro, then connect those same items at Meso. Selecting an item or connection opens its details. The Notebook sits beside the canvas on wide screens and replaces it on phones until you switch back.

The Notebook selector switches between Memo and suggestions and Field Notes. Select a map item to open its details and interpretation. Edit Label or Relationship, then choose Save or Cancel; moving focus does not save a draft. Saving wording preserves positions and the current view. Switching views preserves your work and makes no model request.

The Level Selector

The top-bar selector names the level and its state, such as Micro · complete. You can edit completed levels; the next locked level opens after you complete its predecessor. A new level without work requires an initial reading. Existing saved work does not require regeneration.

Map Tools

Open Map tools to change the view or layout. Canvas shows the drawing; List presents the same items and relationships in a table for review or screen-reader use. Find searches labels. Add item creates items where the level allows them; at Meso, connect existing Micro items instead.

On wide screens, the Map tools panel stays anchored at the upper right. Drag its left edge to resize the whole panel. With Resize map tools focused, Left widens it and Right narrows it; hold Shift for larger steps. Home or a double-click restores the default width, and End uses the widest available size. The browser remembers the width. On narrow screens, the panel stays compact and hides the resize handle.

The minus and plus buttons change the zoom. Fit map brings every visible item into the window, and the 0 key does the same when the canvas has focus. Arrange map lays out the items automatically. You can undo layout changes from the current visit with Ctrl/Cmd+Z.

Map key explains the level colors and line styles. Full screen map closes the Notebook and fills the viewport with the map. Press Esc to leave full screen.

Project Menu

The Project menu includes Projects, which opens the list of maps in your workspace, and Progress, which shows the stages, levels, and project link. Course students can use Submit revision in the Notebook to record their progress. To share the map itself, send a project link or export.

Export & share offers the links and files described in Export & Sharing. Preferences includes display options and Automatic suggestion addition. When that setting is on, eligible suggestions enter the map for you to review.

The Five Stages

In 1. Add memo, write your question and add the memo. In 2. Memo review, choose Generate guidance and check the suggestions against the memo. New levels without work also need an initial reading. In 3. Choose elements, decide which items or relationships to add.

In 4. Build map, place Micro items, connect them at Meso, and extend the map at Exo and Macro. Record evidence as you work. In 5. Review and export, answer Field Notes and inspect the level. You can run Map Check, export, or record a saved course revision. Share a link or export separately when the instructor needs the content.

Footer

To send a problem report, choose Report a problem in the footer and enter your description. The report includes your verified account name and the app release, but excludes memo text, map content, and Field Notes.
