Map and Notebook

Use Map and Notebook at the top right to move between the map and the Notebook, where you can review suggestions, add notes, and edit details. You can add items at Micro and connect them at Meso, then select an item or connection to open its details. On a wide screen, the Notebook sits beside the map; on a phone, it replaces the map until you switch back.

In the Notebook, you can switch between Memo submission and Field Notes. Select an item on the map to open its details and interpretation. Edit Label or Relationship, then choose Save or Cancel; moving focus does not save a draft. Saving wording preserves positions and the current view. Switching views preserves your work and makes no model request.

The Level Selector

The selector at the top shows your current level and its state, such as Micro · complete. You can return to completed levels to edit them, and the next locked level opens when you complete the one before it. The app needs an initial reading for a new level without work, but you can open existing saved work without another reading.

Tools

Open Tools to change the view or layout. Canvas shows the map, while List presents the same items and relationships in a table you can review with a screen reader. Use Find to search labels. When Add item is available, you can use it to add an item to the map. At Meso, connect items from Micro; if one is missing, you can add it to Micro from there.

On wide screens, drag the left edge of the Tools panel to resize it. With Resize map tools focused, Left widens it and Right narrows it; hold Shift for larger steps. Home or a double-click restores the default width, and End uses the widest available size. The browser remembers the width. On narrow screens, the panel stays compact and hides the resize handle.

The minus and plus buttons change the zoom. Fit map brings every visible item into the window, and the 0 key does the same when the canvas has focus. Arrange map lays out the items automatically. You can undo layout changes from the current visit with Ctrl/Cmd+Z.

Open Map key to see what the colors and line styles mean. Full screen map closes the Notebook and fills the window with the map; press Esc to leave full screen.

Project Menu

The Project menu includes Projects, which opens the list of maps in your workspace, and Progress, which shows the stages, levels, and project link. Students working in a course can use Submit revision in the Notebook to record their progress. To share the map itself, send a project link or export.

Export & share offers the links and files described in Export & Sharing. Preferences includes display options and Add suggestions automatically. When that setting is on, eligible suggestions enter the map for you to review.

The Five Stages

In 1. Add memo, write your question and add the memo. In 2. Memo review, choose Generate guidance and check the suggestions against the memo. New levels without work also need an initial reading. In 3. Choose elements, decide which items or relationships to add.

In 4. Build map, place Micro items and connect them at Meso. At Exo, add institutions, resources, policies, and other broader conditions; at Macro, connect larger-scale items to each other and to earlier items. Record evidence as you work. In 5. Review and export, answer Field Notes and inspect the level. You can export your work or record a saved course revision. Share a link or export separately when the instructor needs the content.

Report a Problem

To send a problem report while working on a map, choose the bug icon and enter your description. Elsewhere in the app, choose Report a problem in the footer. The report includes your verified account name and the app release, but excludes memo text, map content, and Field Notes.
