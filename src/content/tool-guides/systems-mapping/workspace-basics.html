Map and Notebook

Use Map and Notebook at the top edge of the Notebook panel to move between the map and the Notebook, where you can review suggestions, add notes, and edit details. When the Notebook is closed, a compact overlay on the map offers the same controls. You can add items at Micro and connect them at Meso, then select an item or connection to open its details. On a wide screen, the Notebook sits beside the map, and on a phone, it replaces the map until you switch back.

In the Notebook, you can switch between Memo submission and Field Notes. Select an item on the map to open its evidence passage and Interpretation. Edit Label or Relationship, then choose Save or Cancel. Moving focus does not save a draft. Saving wording preserves positions and the current view. Switching views preserves your work and makes no model request. Choose Done to close the details.

The Level Selector

The level selector sits beside Tools and shows your current level, such as Micro. You can return to completed levels to edit them, and the next locked level opens when you complete the one before it. Once course guidance is ready, the app reads the memo once for each new level without work, and existing saved work opens without another reading.

Tools

Open Tools to change the view or layout. Canvas shows the map, whereas List presents the same items and relationships in a table you can review with a screen reader. Use the search field to find labels. When Add item is available, you can use it to add an item to the map. At Meso, connect items from Micro. If one is missing, you can add it to Micro from there.

On wide screens, drag the left edge of the Tools panel to resize it. With Resize map tools focused, ← widens it and → narrows it, and you can hold Shift for larger steps. Home or a double-click restores the default width, and End uses the widest available size. The browser remembers the width. On narrow screens, the panel stays compact and hides the resize handle.

The minus and plus buttons change the zoom. Fit map brings every visible item into the window, and 0 does the same when the canvas has focus. Arrange map lays out the items automatically. Press Ctrl/Cmd+Z to undo layout changes from the current visit.

Open Map key to see the colors and line styles. Full screen map closes the Notebook and fills the window with the map. Press Esc to leave full screen.

Project Menu

The Project menu includes Projects, which opens the list of maps in your workspace, and Progress, which shows the stages, levels, and project link. Submit revision in the Notebook's Field Notes section records your progress and does not send your map. Share a project link or an exported copy when your instructor needs to read it.

Export & share offers the links and files described in Export & Sharing. Preferences includes display options and Add suggestions automatically. When that setting is on, eligible suggestions enter the map for you to review.

The Five Stages

In 1. Add memo, write your question and add the memo. In 2. Memo review, choose Generate guidance and check the suggestions against the memo. New levels without work also need an initial reading. In 3. Choose elements, decide which items or relationships to add.

In 4. Build map, place Micro items and connect them at Meso. At Exo, add institutions, resources, policies, and other broader conditions, and at Macro, connect larger-scale items to each other and to earlier items. Add evidence and Interpretation as you work. In 5. Review and export, answer Field Notes and inspect the level. You can export your work or choose Submit revision to record your progress.

Report a Problem

To report a problem while working on a map, choose the bug icon in the main header and enter your description. Elsewhere in the app, choose Report a problem in the footer. The report includes your verified account name, the page, and the app release, and excludes memo text, map content, and Field Notes.
