What Instructors Can See

Your instructor can see your course progress and how many revisions you have submitted. Submit revision records your progress and does not send your map. Share a project link or an exported copy when your instructor needs to read it. Maps in your general workspace remain separate from your course work.

Storage and Retention

The app saves up to 200 maps per workspace to your account and checks revisions before accepting changes. It also stores a recovery copy in the browser. Anyone using the same browser profile can read the recovery copy, and signing in with CUNY Login does not encrypt it.

The app retains guidance requests and results for 24 hours so it can recover them after a reload. In the separate review queue, it retains material awaiting feedback for 30 days and material with feedback for 180 days. These periods do not apply to the progress record created by Submit revision.

Project links expire after 90 days, and you can revoke a link sooner when the people who received it no longer need access.

Model Limits

Requests go through the Lab's model gateway. If guidance fails, read the message and follow its instructions. The app may ask you to reopen the map to check whether the guidance is ready.

See Memo & Suggestions for the limits on suggestions. The model may suggest fewer items or none. A model request has a size limit, so you may need to shorten a long memo before requesting guidance.

Read the transfer disclosure before the first request. Requests include course guidance and materials, whereas instructor identity and prompt history stay out of the request.

What to Put in a Memo

The app saves your memo with the map and sends it to the model when you request guidance. Remove identifying details from fieldwork before pasting it, and follow your course's confidentiality rules. Exports are created in your browser, so check a copy before sharing it.

Report a Problem

To report a problem while working on a map, choose the bug icon in the main header and enter your description. Elsewhere in the app, choose Report a problem in the footer. The report includes your verified account name, the page, and the app release, and excludes memo text, map content, and Field Notes. For access questions, email ailab@gc.cuny.edu.
