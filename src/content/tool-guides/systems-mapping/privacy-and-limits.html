What Instructors Can See

Your instructor can see your course progress and how many revisions you have submitted, but Submit revision does not send them your map. If they need to read it, share a project link or export. Maps in your general workspace remain separate from your course work.

Storage and Retention

The app saves up to 200 maps per workspace to your account and checks revisions before accepting changes. It also keeps a recovery copy in the browser. Anyone who uses the same browser profile can read that browser storage, which CUNY Login does not encrypt.

Guidance requests and results are kept for 24 hours so the app can recover them after a reload. In the separate review queue, material awaiting feedback is kept for 30 days, and material with feedback is kept for 180 days. These periods do not describe the progress record created by Submit revision.

Project links expire after 90 days, and you can revoke a link sooner when the people who received it no longer need access.

Model Limits

Requests go through the Lab's model gateway. If a request fails, read the message and follow its instructions. The app may ask you to reopen the map to check whether the guidance is ready.

Memo & Suggestions explains the limits on suggestions. The model may suggest fewer items or none. The app also limits the size of a request and will ask you to shorten the material if it is too large.

Read the transfer disclosure before the first request. Requests include course guidance and materials, while instructor identity and prompt history stay out of the request.

What to Put in a Memo

The app saves your memo with the map and sends it to the model when you request a review. Remove identifying details from fieldwork before pasting it, and follow your course's confidentiality rules. Exports are created in your browser; check a copy before sharing it. Problem reports do not include memo text or map content.

Reporting a Problem

To report a problem, choose Report a problem in the footer and enter your description in the form’s single field. The report includes your verified account name, the page, and the app release. For access questions, email ailab@gc.cuny.edu.
