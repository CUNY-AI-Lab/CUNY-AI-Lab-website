Add a Memo

Start with a short question about the person or group you want to understand, then paste or upload a memo. You might use field notes, a case description, an interview transcript, or a reading. A model request has a size limit, so you may need to shorten a long memo before requesting guidance. If the request is too large, your saved memo remains unchanged.

When you upload a PDF, the app extracts selectable text. Check the imported text against the original and paste any missing passages. If no text can be read, paste the memo or upload a DOCX. The app also accepts DOCX, TXT, Markdown, HTML, and JSON memo files, and uploads must be no larger than 5 MB.

Before the first request, read the transfer disclosure. It describes the memo, question, current level, and published course guidance that the app sends with the request. You can use a different memo at each level. When the app reads a memo for the next level, it also receives your Field Notes from the preceding level, separate from the memo. For a long note, it receives an opening excerpt, and the full note remains saved.

Review Suggestions

The suggestions appear in the Notebook. At Micro, they are items you might add to the map, and at Meso, they are relationships between items you have already added. Open Details to read the source passage and the model's reasoning, and check the memo yourself when a suggestion has no passage.

For each suggestion, choose Add to map, Set aside, or Remove. Once you have decided on every suggestion, choose Finish review. If you want the app to read the memo again later, use Update suggestions → Reread memo. Saving your edits does not request another reading.

Under Project → Preferences, use Add suggestions automatically to choose whether eligible suggestions are added to the map. You can review and remove them there.

What the Model Sends Back

The model may suggest fewer items or connections than the limits below, or none at all. It can also offer up to three pieces of guidance.

LevelMaximum suggestions per review
Micro10 items, no relationships.
Meso8 relationships between existing Micro items, no new items.
Exo6 larger-scale items, no relationships.
Macro8 relationships between existing items, no new items.

The app checks quotations against the memo and accepts differences such as straight and curly quotation marks. You should still read each passage and decide whether it supports the suggestion.

If guidance fails, read the message and follow its instructions. The app may retry some requests. If it asks you to reopen the map, check whether the guidance is ready before starting another request.

If the app can show feedback but cannot use the map suggestions, it preserves the feedback for you to check against your memo. You can add to the map yourself or use Update suggestions → Reread memo to try again. Your previous map work remains unchanged.

Guidance and Evidence

Guidance and evidence shows feedback from the model and any passages it cites from your memo. Read the passages in context and decide whether they support the feedback. The app also sends published course guidance to the model with its instructions when you request guidance.

Initial Guidance and Later Manual Work

To begin a new map, choose Generate guidance and wait for a response. You can continue even if it returns no suggestions. If the request fails, choose Try again. Once course guidance is ready, the app reads the memo once for each new level without work, and existing saved work opens without another reading. After that, you can add and revise items yourself.
