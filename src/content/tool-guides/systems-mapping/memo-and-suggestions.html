Adding a Memo

Start with a short question about the person or group you want to understand, then paste or upload a memo. You might use field notes, a case description, an interview, or a reading. A model request has a size limit, so you may need to shorten a long memo before requesting guidance. If the request is rejected for its size, your saved project remains unchanged.

When you upload a PDF, the app extracts selectable text. Check the imported text against the original and paste any missing passages. If no text can be read, paste the memo or upload a DOCX. The app also accepts DOCX, TXT, Markdown, HTML, and JSON memo files; uploads must be no larger than 5 MB.

Before the first request, read the transfer disclosure. It describes the memo, question, current level, and published course guidance that will be sent with the request. You can use a different memo at each level. When the app reads the next memo, it also receives your earlier Field Notes as your notes, separate from the source.

Reviewing Suggestions

The suggestions appear in the Notebook. At Micro, they are items you might add to the map; at Meso, they are relationships between items you have already added. Open Details to read the source passage and the model's reasoning, and check the memo yourself when a suggestion has no passage.

For each suggestion, choose Add to map, Set aside, or Remove. Once you have decided on every suggestion, choose Finish review. If you want the app to read the memo again later, use Update suggestions → Reread memo; saving your edits does not request another reading.

Under Project → Preferences, you can turn on Automatic suggestion addition. The app then adds eligible suggestions to the map, where you can review and remove them. The setting is off by default.

What the Model Sends Back

The model may suggest fewer items or connections than the limits below, or none at all. It can also offer up to three pieces of guidance.

LevelMaximum suggestions per review
Micro10 items; no relationships.
Meso8 relationships between existing Micro items; no new items.
Exo6 items and 5 relationships.
Macro6 items and 12 links to earlier items; no relationships.

The app checks quotations against the memo and accepts differences such as straight and curly quotation marks. You should still read each passage and decide whether it supports the suggestion.

If guidance fails, read the message and follow its instructions. The app may retry some requests. If it asks you to reopen the map, check whether the guidance is ready before starting another request.

Guidance and Evidence

Guidance and evidence shows the material your instructor has published for the current Part and stage. The model receives that material along with the app's fixed instructions when you request guidance.

Initial Guidance and Later Manual Work

To begin a new map, choose Generate guidance and wait for a response; you can continue even if it returns no suggestions. If the request fails, use Try again. The app also needs to read the memo when you start a level that has no work yet, once course guidance is ready. After that, you can add and revise items yourself, and existing saved work remains available without another reading.
