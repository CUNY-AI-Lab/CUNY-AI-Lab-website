Your Memos

In Memo, you can select the part you want to work on. Part 1 · Local people and places supports Micro and Meso. Part 2 · Institutions and broader conditions supports Exo and Macro. The second memo has its own text and upload field. Your first memo and existing map remain saved when you begin it.

Upload a PDF, DOCX, or text file up to 5 MB, or paste the text. The upload control also accepts Markdown, HTML, and JSON memo files. For PDFs, the app extracts selectable text. Compare the result with the original and paste missing passages. A scanned PDF may require text extraction before you can use it.

Submit a Memo

When the text is ready, you can choose Submit memo and add nodes in Memo. The app sends the active memo with the context needed to identify supported nodes. Each submission can add up to ten nodes. Part 1 adds local nodes, and Part 2 divides its nodes between outside settings at Exo and broader conditions at Macro. It may add fewer nodes or none when the source provides insufficient evidence, and Notebook lists any proposals it left out under Left out.

Nodes are added directly to the map. Select each one to read its source quotation and edit its label, System, Type, or interpretation. You create all relationships yourself. Use Continue after a successful submission to advance through the mapping steps.

Update Your Memo

You can return to Memo to revise either source. After you revise the memo, the notice Memo changed | click Update map to submit identifies an unsubmitted draft. Choose Update map when you are ready for the app to process it.

An update uses changed passages and existing node information to add supported nodes or update source passages on generated nodes where your edits allow it. Your edited labels, classifications, evidence, positions, notes, and connections are preserved. Removing a passage from the memo does not automatically remove its node, and resubmitting does not restore a node you removed.

Whitespace-only and bibliography-only edits can be saved without another model request. Switching between memos, reopening a project, and saving ordinary edits also leave the last submitted analysis unchanged. The two memos have separate drafts and submitted versions.

Read the Evidence

Open a node and read Quote from memo. Compare it with the source and decide whether it supports the classification. The app checks that quotations match submitted text, but you still need to judge what the passage establishes and what remains uncertain.

Submission Problems

Read the message and follow any retry instructions. Your current memo draft and saved map remain available. If you change the source while a request is running, an outdated response cannot replace your newer work. Check which memo is selected before trying again.

If Continue is unavailable, open Memo and check whether the current part still needs submission or Update map. Part 2 needs a previously successful Part 1 submission. A later draft edit to Part 1 does not erase an already completed Part 2 submission.
