Get Access

Instructors request a course through the CUNY AI Lab, then invite students with the Lab's class invitation. Once the Lab approves the course for Systems Mapping, it appears under Your courses on Projects. Course owners can open the Instructor Console there.

Accept your instructor's invitation, then open Systems Mapping through the Lab portal or systems-mapping.ailab-452.workers.dev. Sign in with CUNY Login and choose your course. Membership is checked on each request. Course maps stay separate from the general workspace, where Lab members can keep projects without joining a course.

First Sign-In

Use a recent browser on a computer or phone; phones have a compact toolbar. Bring a memo such as field notes, a case description, an interview transcript, or a reading. A model request has a size limit, so you may need to shorten a long memo before generating guidance; your saved memo remains unchanged if the request is too large.

The sign-in page shows the four levels and CUNY Login. After signing in, choose New project on Projects. Each project has one map. Write a short question naming the person or group you want to understand, then paste or upload the memo.

Your First Micro Level

In the Notebook's Memo review, choose Generate guidance. The button shows Reading memo… while the app works. At Micro, the model can suggest people, places, and other items from the memo. It may suggest fewer than ten items or none. After the reading finishes, review the suggestions and use Add item in Map tools to add anything missing.

Open Details on each suggestion and compare its passage with the proposed item. Choose Add to map when you can support it, Set aside to revisit it, or Remove to reject it. After every suggestion has a decision, choose Finish review. Arrange the items and select them to edit interpretation and evidence. Relationship arrows come at Meso, between existing Micro items.

Answer the instructor's Micro question under Field Notes, then choose Continue to Meso-level. Micro items keep their identities and positions. A new level without work needs its own reading, which can begin once course guidance is ready. Existing saved work remains available without regeneration.

Compare Your Reading with the Model's

Before choosing Generate guidance, write down people and places you expect to find in the memo. Compare your list with the suggestions, then check the memo if you disagree with a suggestion or notice something missing.

Choose a Manageable First Memo

Begin with about a page and name the person your question concerns. Check each item against the memo. Use Set aside for a suggestion you want to revisit.

Continue Your Map

The Four Levels explains what changes when you move to Meso, Exo, and Macro. Workspace Basics locates the controls you will use along the way. Instructors can use Instructor Console to prepare Field Notes questions and guidance before students begin.

Privacy and Saved Work

Maps save to your account, with a browser recovery copy. Instructors see course progress and revision counts, not map content from Submit revision. Share a project link or export when they need to read the work. Read Privacy & Limits before adding sensitive material.
