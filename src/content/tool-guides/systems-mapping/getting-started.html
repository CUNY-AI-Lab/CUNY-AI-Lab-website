Get Access

Instructors request a course through the CUNY AI Lab and invite students using the Lab's class invitation. Once the course is approved for Systems Mapping, you can find it under Classes and materials in Account. Instructors can open the Instructor Console there.

Accept your instructor's invitation, then open Systems Mapping through the Lab portal or systems-mapping.ailab-452.workers.dev. Sign in with CUNY Login and choose your course. The app checks your course membership when you make a request. Course maps stay separate from the general workspace, where Lab members can keep projects without joining a course.

First Sign-In

Use a recent browser on a computer or phone; phones have a compact toolbar. Bring a memo such as field notes, a case description, an interview transcript, or a reading. A model request has a size limit, so you may need to shorten a long memo before generating guidance; your saved memo remains unchanged if the request is too large.

After signing in, choose New project on the Projects page. Each project has one map. Write a short question about the person or group you want to understand, then paste or upload your memo.

As you save projects, they appear on the Projects page. Select a title to reopen its map, or use the search and menus to find projects by name, order them by name or recent activity, or filter them by level.

Your First Micro Level

In the Notebook's Memo review, choose Generate guidance. While the app reads your memo, the button shows Reading memo…. At Micro, it can suggest people, places, and other items for the map, though it may suggest fewer than ten or none at all. Once it finishes, review what it found. When Add item is available in Tools, you can use it to add anything missing.

Open Details on each suggestion and compare its passage with the proposed item. Choose Add to map when you can support it, Set aside to revisit it, or Remove to reject it. After every suggestion has a decision, choose Finish review. Arrange the items, then select each one to add or edit your interpretation and evidence. At Meso, you can draw arrows between the items you added at Micro.

Answer your instructor's Micro question under Field Notes, then choose Continue to Meso-level. The items you added stay in place as you begin to connect them. Once course guidance is ready, the app needs to read the memo at each new level that has no work yet; existing saved work remains available without another reading.

Compare Your Reading with the Model's

Before choosing Generate guidance, write down people and places you expect to find in the memo. Compare your list with the suggestions, then check the memo if you disagree with a suggestion or notice something missing.

Choose a Manageable First Memo

You might start with a memo about a page long and a question about the person you want to understand. Check each item against the memo. Use Set aside for a suggestion you want to revisit.

Continue Your Map

The Four Levels explains what changes when you move to Meso, Exo, and Macro. Workspace Basics locates the controls you will use along the way. Instructors can use Instructor Console to prepare Field Notes questions and guidance before students begin.

Privacy and Saved Work

Maps save to your account, with a browser recovery copy. Instructors see course progress and revision counts, not map content from Submit revision. Share a project link or export when they need to read the work. Read Privacy & Limits before adding sensitive material.
