Items and Relationships

To rename an item, select it and open the Notebook, then edit its Label. To change a connection, select it and edit Relationship. Choose Save to save your changes or Cancel to return to the saved wording. You can also press Enter to save and Esc to cancel. Moving focus or selecting another item does not save a draft, and the same controls appear in suggestion Details.

The label must fit the item’s existing level. For a relationship at Meso, enter a verb or phrase. If a draft is not valid, the app preserves the saved wording and shows the needed change. Saving also updates any matching suggestion, and renaming an item updates its name in connected relationships.

You cannot change an item's level directly. To move it, resolve any connections that depend on it, choose Delete at the end of its details, and add it at the appropriate level. At Meso, connect existing Micro items. Choose Connect from [item name], select another item, and describe the relationship and its direction. Then use Interpretation to explain what the relationship shows.

Drag items to arrange them on the map, or use the arrows under Position on map in an item's details. The app saves their positions with the project. At Meso, add arrows between the items from Micro. Open Tools → Map key to see the colors and line styles. Solid arrows show relationships, and dotted lines show other connections, such as where an item belongs.

List View

When you want to inspect labels or find an incomplete relationship, open Tools → List. The table shows each item's level and settings alongside relationships and their verbs. You can review the same map without navigating the canvas, including when you use a keyboard or screen reader.

Layout and Undo

Saving a label or relationship leaves items in place, although a card may change size. Move an item yourself or choose Arrange map to arrange the map automatically. Press Ctrl/Cmd+Z to undo moves and arrangements from the current visit. Undo does not reverse changes to labels or evidence.

Choose Fit map to bring the visible items, connection labels, and relationship fields into view. Empty ring areas may fall outside the window so the work itself fits.

Removing Items with Connections

You cannot delete an item or change a Micro setting to another type while other parts of the map depend on it. Resolve the listed connections first, then try again.
