About Systems Mapping

Systems Mapping helps you turn a research memo into a visual map of the people, organizations, resources, and policies connected to a community issue. As you build your map, you can identify relationships, consider barriers and assets, and explore how different parts of a system connect.

Starting with the people and settings closest to the person in your memo, you build outward through four levels. You connect the people and places you have mapped, add larger institutions, resources, policies, and conditions, and then explore how they relate to one another. The app can suggest what to add based on your memo, and you decide what belongs on the map.

Systems Mapping was developed for the Critical AI Literacy Institute. The CUNY AI Lab hosts it.
