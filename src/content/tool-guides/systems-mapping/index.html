About Systems Mapping

Maria Mavrides Calderon proposed Systems Mapping for a course on the systems that shape the lives of young people and families in New York City. Students turn a research memo into a network graph, beginning with the people and places involved in a local issue. As they build the map, they connect families, schools, community organizations, and public agencies to the laws, policies, funding decisions, and broader historical and social conditions that shape what each can do.

The project draws on ecological systems theory and asset mapping to help students see both the barriers people face and the resources they already draw on. A map might show how eligibility rules limit access to a program, alongside the informal networks families rely on to find support. Students use evidence from their research to decide which relationships to draw, whose knowledge the map represents, and where they need to learn more. The map can then help them identify where a community advocacy project might make a difference.

Systems Mapping was developed through the Critical AI Literacy Institute, and the CUNY AI Lab hosts it.
