About Systems Mapping

Systems Mapping helps you make a map from a memo, such as a case study, interview, or field note. You can review the app's suggestions and decide what belongs on your map.

The map has four levels. At Micro, add people and places from the memo. At Meso, connect them. At Exo, show how decisions made elsewhere affect them. At Macro, add broader conditions such as laws and policies.

To start building a map, add a memo and choose Generate guidance. The app needs to read a memo again when you start a level that has no work yet. After that, you can edit the map yourself and ask for more suggestions if you want them.

Systems Mapping was developed for the Critical AI Literacy Institute. The CUNY AI Lab hosts it.
