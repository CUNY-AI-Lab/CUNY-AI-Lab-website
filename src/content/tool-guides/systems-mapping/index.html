About Systems Mapping

Systems Mapping turns a memo into a map you can explain and revise. Begin at Micro with the people, groups, roles, activities, objects, resources, and immediate settings relevant to a focal person. At Meso, connect those existing items with relationship arrows. Exo traces outside decisions, and Macro examines broader conditions. The work stays in one cumulative map.

Systems Mapping was developed as part of the tinkering track of the Critical AI Literacy Institute, from spring 2026 through fall 2026. Its memo → map → Field Notes workflow asks students to choose what belongs on the map, support those choices with evidence, and identify what to investigate next.

A new map requires an initial model reading, as does a new level without existing work. Students decide what to keep. After that reading, they can build and revise through their own analysis. Existing saved work remains available without regeneration; later rereading and Map Check are optional.
