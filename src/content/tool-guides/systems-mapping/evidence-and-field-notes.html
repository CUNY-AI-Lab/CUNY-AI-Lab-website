Read and Add Evidence

Select an item or relationship on the map to open it in the Notebook. Its complete evidence passage appears as a quotation below its name, and No source evidence recorded. appears when it has none. Read the passage in context and consider whether it supports what you have put on the map.

When you add an item, put a passage or question in Evidence or question. Use Interpretation to answer the question above the field, such as what the item helps explain and what is still uncertain, then choose Save note and check the status beside it. Maps with earlier evidence fields show them under Saved evidence and context.

Field Notes

Each level asks you to answer one question your instructor publishes. The default question asks what you noticed at the current level and which items could help you begin the next one. A response is required to complete the level. Choose Save note and check the status beside it. If saving fails, the app shows a message so you can try again. You can return to the Map while writing.

If you have written notes at other levels, open Previous notes to read them. Your answers remain there even if your instructor changes a question. When the app reads a memo for the next level, it also receives your Field Notes from the preceding level, separate from the memo. For a long note, it receives an opening excerpt, and the full note remains saved.

Map Note

Use Map note in the Notebook's Field Notes section to write about something you notice or want to investigate across the map. Choose Save note and check the status beside it. If saving fails, the app shows a message so you can try again.

Write Field Notes

You can use Field Notes to write down what you want to learn next. For example, if a fictional memo describes Rosa's hospital visits, you might ask who decides when her appointments take place and explore that question at Exo.

When you refer to an item on your map, use its label so someone reading a shared map or exported copy can find it. Submit revision records your progress and does not send your map or Field Notes. Share a project link or an exported copy when your instructor needs to read them.
