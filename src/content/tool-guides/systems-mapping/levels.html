The Progression

The four levels help you look at a situation from different perspectives. For example, if a fictional memo follows Rosa to a hospital appointment, you might place the hospital at Micro because she goes there and its billing office at Exo if its decisions affect her without her participation.

Start with the people, places, and other details in the memo, then connect them at Meso. At Exo, add larger-scale institutions, resources, policies, and conditions. At Macro, connect those items to each other and to the local map. Relationship fields also allow you to describe how a connection changes over time.

Part 1, Micro and Meso

Micro

Map the people and places connected to the person in the memo. You can also add groups, roles, activities, objects, and resources when the memo supports them. For a memo about a hospital visit, you might add the person, a nurse, and the hospital as separate items, with evidence for each. Add relationship arrows at Meso.

Meso

Select a Micro item, choose Connect from [item name] in its details, then select a different Micro item. Name the relationship, choose the direction of the arrow, and use Interpretation to explain what the relationship shows. If an item is missing and adding items is available, you can add it to Micro from Meso and then connect it. For example, if a nurse refers Rosa to a neighborhood center, use the memo to describe that connection and decide which way the arrow should point.

Part 2, Exo and Macro

Exo

Add larger-scale institutions, resources, policies, or conditions that help explain the local map. For example, you might add a billing office or a parent's employer and record what the memo says about it. You can connect these items at Macro.

Macro

Connect the larger-scale items you added at Exo to one another or to items from the local map. Describe each relationship, choose its direction if you know it, and use the memo to explain what supports it.

How Work Carries Forward

The map builds on your earlier work. Your items stay in place when you connect them at Meso, and the larger-scale items you add at Exo stay on the map when you connect them at Macro. You can also describe how a relationship changes over time.

Before you continue from Meso, the Review Micro and Meso dialog offers Review the map and Continue to Exo-level. Check for omissions and unsupported relationships before continuing. Once course guidance is ready, the app reads the memo once for each new level without work, and existing saved work opens without another reading.

When you open an older map, the app moves earlier Micro relationships to Meso.

Common Placement Questions

A classroom can appear at Micro for a student who attends it. A district office can appear at Exo when an attendance decision affects that student indirectly. A parent's workplace can also affect a child, for example when a shift change alters the family's schedule.

At Exo, you might add a housing court, a landlord, or a housing policy that affects the person in the memo. At Macro, connect those larger-scale items to each other or to the local map when the evidence supports the connection.
