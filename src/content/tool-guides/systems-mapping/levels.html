The Progression

A hospital belongs at Micro when Rosa participates in appointments there. Its billing office can belong at Exo when decisions reach her without her participation. The focal person's participation guides placement.

Micro identifies items and immediate-setting membership. Meso connects those items in the same map space. Exo traces outside decisions, and Macro identifies broader conditions. Relationship fields describe process and change over time.

Part 1, Micro and Meso

Micro

Map the people and immediate settings relevant to the focal person. Groups, roles, activities, objects, and resources also belong when the memo supports them. Add Rosa, Nurse Jo, the hospital, and the neighborhood center as separate items, each with evidence. A non-setting item can identify where it appears. Add relationship arrows at Meso.

Meso

Select a Micro item, choose Connect from [node name] in its details, then select a different Micro item. Name the relationship, record evidence, and choose a one-way or two-way arrow. For example, Nurse Jo “refers” Rosa; the hospital may exchange paperwork with the neighborhood center. Let the source establish direction rather than assuming both ways.

Part 2, Exo and Macro

Exo

Map an outside setting whose decisions affect the focal person without their participation, such as a billing office or a parent's employer. Trace the effect into a Micro setting. Include an intermediate outside office when the evidence supports that step.

Macro

Identify a broader condition, such as a law, policy, economic condition, history, or belief. Scope it to at least two earlier elements and explain each connection through evidence. Place an organization according to its role in the case; use Macro for the broader condition.

How Work Carries Forward

Micro items retain their IDs and positions when Meso relationships connect them. Macro scope links record influence on earlier elements without creating a directional relationship. Meso and Exo relationships can describe frequency, duration, direction, conditions, and change over time.

Before you continue from Meso, the Review Micro and Meso dialog lets you choose Review the map or Continue to Exo-level. Check for omissions and unsupported relationships before continuing. A new level without work needs its own reading once course guidance is ready; existing saved maps remain available without regeneration.

Older Micro relationships move to Meso with their IDs and content intact. Older scoped Meso records remain readable for compatibility, but are not the current creation workflow.

Common Placement Questions

A classroom belongs at Micro for a student who attends it. A district office can belong at Exo when its attendance decision reaches that student indirectly. Apply the same test to a parent's workplace: a shift change can affect a child who does not participate there.

Housing insecurity is a Macro condition. A housing court or landlord may belong at Exo when the case traces an outside decision. At Meso, connect two different existing Micro items and support the wording and direction with evidence. The endpoints need not both be settings.
