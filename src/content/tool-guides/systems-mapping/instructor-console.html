Open the Console

Open Account, choose your course, and select Instructor Console. Course owners and specifically authorized guest instructors can prepare questions and guidance within their permissions. Use Back to project or Projects to return to student work.

Prepare Optional Field Notes Questions

In Course settings, choose Edit prompts, select a level, and write a question of up to 1,200 characters. You might ask what a source supports, whose perspective is missing, or what students want to investigate next. Field Notes are optional in the app, so explain any course requirements in your assignment.

Student preview shows the current question, including unsaved edits. Save draft saves it for later. Publish changes saves pending edits and publishes the questions. The editor checks that each level has a question. Existing student notes remain available when a question changes.

Guidance and Materials

Under Guidance and materials, choose the Part and Mapping step you want to edit. The console retains Part 1 and Part 2 settings, with guidance up to 2,500 characters and course material up to 4,000 characters for each section. Its mapping-step settings are configuration sections, while the student workspace follows Micro, Meso, Exo, and Macro.

Use the saved guidance preview to inspect published course text. Changing the preview level does not change the saved version. Editing and previewing course settings make no model request. In the student workflow, the app reads submitted memos to add supported nodes, and students create the relationships.

Publish and Restore Versions

Published versions remain available in Versions and change history. Create a draft from an earlier version if you want to revise or restore it, then save and publish. Publication applies to later requests and preserves existing student work.

If someone else has changed the settings, the app checks the revision before saving. Read the conflict message and compare your draft with the server version. A failed publication can leave a saved draft available for retry.

Students and Saved Progress

Under Submissions, open Students and saved progress to load the course roster and saved-map information. Use Refresh students and More students when available. These records can show visits and saved progress. Ask students for a project link or exported copy to read their current map, sources, and notes.

The Submissions list may also contain records from the earlier instructor-review process. Keep those records distinct from the current two-memo workflow. The present student Notebook has optional Field Notes and direct progression through the four mapping steps.
