Opening the Console

From a course workspace, open Account and choose Instructor Console to prepare questions and guidance for your course. You can also check course activity under Saved progress or open Submissions to see work submitted through the separate review process. Use Account to sign out or Projects to return to your workspace.

Field Notes Prompts

In Course settings, choose Edit prompts to start a draft from the published version. Select a level and write a question of up to 1,200 characters; you might name the case or reading and ask students what they want to investigate next.

Open Student preview to read the exact student-facing plain text, including unsaved edits. Editing and previewing make no model request. Choose Publish changes to save and publish, or Save draft to return later. Each level needs a question, and the editor will show you if one is missing. Changing a question does not remove students' existing answers.

For example, you could ask whose perspective is missing from a Micro map and which relationship students want to investigate at Meso.

Guidance and Materials

In Guidance and materials, choose a Part and mapping step. Part 1 covers Micro and Meso; Part 2 covers Exo and Macro. For each, you can add up to 2,500 characters of guidance and 4,000 characters of course material.

Use Preview saved guidance to see what students will read without the app's internal prompts. Changing Preview level changes the level used for the preview, but does not change the saved settings. Your course guidance is used alongside the app's fixed instructions for levels, student choice, privacy, and the structure of responses.

Versions

Course settings keep a history of previous versions. Choose Create draft to copy a version for editing, give it a name, and add an optional note when you save it. Publishing applies the settings to new requests. To restore earlier settings, create a draft from that version and publish it. You can still read earlier versions.

When you save, the app checks whether the settings have changed since you opened the draft. If they have, it keeps your draft and offers Load server revision so you can compare them. If saving succeeds but publishing fails, your draft remains saved and you can try publishing again.

Existing Review Submissions

Submissions lists reviews of memos and suggestions, including records from earlier workflows. Open a record, choose a feedback mode, write an annotation or question, and select Record feedback. Feedback does not change the student's map or block progress. Submit revision does not add a review here.

The Submissions list refreshes every 30 seconds and pauses while you write feedback. It shows the material submitted for review, rather than a live view of the current map.

Students and Saved Progress

Open Students and saved progress to load the roster on demand. It shows enrolled students with a recorded visit, latest saved maps, level and stage, and submitted-revision counts. First map pending means no course map has been saved; check access or an unfinished start.

Students use Submit revision in the Notebook's review and Field Notes panel. The app saves their work and records the submission. Ask for a project link or export to read the map, evidence, or Field Notes; revision records do not contain them.
