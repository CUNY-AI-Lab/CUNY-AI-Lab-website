Opening the Console

Choose Instructor Console on the Projects page to prepare questions and guidance for your course. You can also check course activity under Saved progress or open Submissions to see the separate review queue. Use Account to sign out or Projects to return to your workspace.

Field Notes Prompts

In Course settings, choose Edit prompts to start a draft from the published version. Select a level and write a question of up to 1,200 characters; you might name the case or reading and ask students what they want to investigate next.

Open Student preview to read the exact student-facing plain text, including unsaved edits. Editing and previewing make no model request. Choose Publish changes to save and publish, or Save draft to return later. Every scale needs a nonblank question; the editor finds missing ones. Changing a question preserves students' existing answers.

For example, you could ask whose perspective is missing from a Micro map and which relationship students want to investigate at Meso.

Guidance and Materials

In Guidance and materials, choose a Part and mapping step. Part 1 covers Micro and Meso; Part 2 covers Exo and Macro. For each, you can add up to 2,500 characters of guidance and 4,000 characters of course material.

Preview saved guidance shows student-facing text without internal prompts. Preview level changes the preview's analytical context, not the saved configuration. Course guidance follows the app's fixed instructions, so level rules, student choice, privacy, and output structure still govern requests.

Versions

Course settings keep version history. Create draft copies a version for editing; name it and add an optional change note when saving. Publishing applies it to new model requests. To restore earlier settings, draft from that version and publish. Earlier records stay readable.

A save checks the revision. If your draft is stale, the app preserves it and offers Load server revision for comparison. If saving succeeds but publishing fails, the draft remains saved; retry publication.

Existing Review Submissions

Submissions lists reviews of memos and suggestions, including records from earlier workflows. Open a record, choose a feedback mode, write an annotation or question, and select Record feedback. Feedback does not change the student's map or block progress. Submit revision does not add a review here.

The queue refreshes every 30 seconds, pausing while you write feedback. The records show the material submitted for review, rather than a live view of the current map.

Students and Saved Progress

Open Students and saved progress to load the roster on demand. It shows enrolled students with a recorded visit, latest saved maps, level and stage, and submitted-revision counts. First map pending means no course map has been saved; check access or an unfinished start.

Students use Submit revision in the Notebook's review and Field Notes panel. The app saves their work and records the submission. Ask for a project link or export to read the map, evidence, or Field Notes; revision records do not contain them.
