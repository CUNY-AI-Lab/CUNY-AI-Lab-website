Choose a Workspace

After signing in, use the general workspace for your own projects or choose a course you have joined. Each workspace has separate maps and links. Open Account to find courses under Classes and materials. You can also follow a course link from your instructor.

The app checks current membership. Accept the course invitation using the CUNY identity you intend to use for class, and check that you have opened the right workspace before creating a project.

Instructor Access

Course owners can open Instructor Console for their own courses. The console offers course questions, guidance, a roster, and saved-progress information. Access to one course does not grant access to another course.

Saved progress can show that a student has visited and saved a course map, including its saved level and stage. It gives the instructor progress information. Share a project link or exported copy when the instructor needs to read the map, memos, evidence, or notes.

Existing Review Records

The console may contain submissions and revision counts from an earlier review workflow. The current student workspace uses two memos and four direct mapping steps. Use project links or downloads for sharing current work with an instructor.

Guest Instructors and the Demo Course

Guest instructors can receive specific permissions for a course, such as reading the roster or editing prompts. These depend on the permissions the Lab grants. The CAIL Teaching Tools Demo course supports workshops through revocable invitations tied to verified CUNY identities.

When Access Changes

If a course disappears or a link stops opening, check current membership and contact the instructor or Lab. General-workspace maps remain in their original workspace. Changing workspaces does not transfer a project.
