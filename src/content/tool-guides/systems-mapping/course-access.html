Workspaces

Systems Mapping provides a general workspace for signed-in Lab members and a course workspace for each approved course. Each course keeps its own maps, links, and prompts. An item saved in one workspace remains there when you open another.

Students choose a course under Your courses on the Projects page or follow an instructor's course link. The app checks enrollment, so a student who leaves the course loses access even if they retain the link. A course owner can use instructor controls for that course; owning one course does not grant access to another.

What an Instructor Sees

ItemVisible to the course instructor?
Roster and first/latest visitYes, from admission records and the course workspace.
Saved progress and submitted revisionsYes. The instructor sees progress, counts, and timestamps, but not the map itself.
Map content, memo, evidence, Field NotesThrough an eligible project link shared with them, not through Submit revision. You can also share an exported copy separately.
Map content without a shared link or copyNo. Recording a revision does not give the instructor access to the map.
General-workspace mapsNot through the instructor role. General-workspace project-link rules still apply.

Use the roster to check who has started and which level they have reached. Submit revision records progress without sending the map. Ask students for a project link or exported copy if you need to read it. The separate Submissions queue may include reviews from an earlier workflow.

Guest Instructors

A course can grant a guest instructor specific permissions to open the instructor panel, read the roster, or read and edit prompts. The grant does not require course ownership, student membership, or administrator access. The app checks the relevant permission on every request.

Demo Course

The CAIL Teaching Tools Demo course supports workshops and try-outs. The Lab issues revocable invitations tied to verified CUNY identities. Demo maps follow the same privacy rules as maps in other courses.
