Workspaces

When you sign in to Systems Mapping, you can use the general workspace or choose a course workspace for a course you have joined. Each workspace has its own maps, links, and prompts, so a map you save in one will not move when you open another.

Students can open Account and choose a course under Classes and materials, or follow a link from their instructor. The app checks enrollment, so students who leave a course lose access even if they still have the link. Course owners can use the instructor controls for courses they own, and owning one course does not grant access to another.

What an Instructor Sees

ItemVisible to the course instructor?
Roster and first sign-inYes, from admission records and the course workspace.
Saved progress and submitted revisionsYes. The instructor sees progress, counts, and timestamps, but not the map itself.
Map content, memo, evidence, Field NotesThrough an eligible project link or an exported copy you share. Submit revision does not send them.
Map content without a shared link or copyNo. Submit revision does not send your map.
General-workspace mapsNot through the instructor role. General-workspace project-link rules still apply.

Instructors can use the roster to see who has started and how far they have gotten. Submit revision records a student's progress and does not send the map, so ask for a project link or an exported copy when you need to read it. The separate Submissions list may include reviews from an earlier workflow.

Guest Instructors

A guest instructor can have permission to open the instructor panel, read the roster, or read and edit prompts without being a course owner, student, or administrator. The app checks these permissions on each request.

Demo Course

The CAIL Teaching Tools Demo course supports workshops and try-outs. The Lab issues revocable invitations tied to verified CUNY identities. Demo maps follow the same privacy rules as maps in other courses.
