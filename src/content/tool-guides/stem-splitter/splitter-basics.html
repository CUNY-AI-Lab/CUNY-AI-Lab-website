Main Tabs

After signing in, you can use Splitter to import audio and open recordings in the Session Rack, Workspace for your courses, and Settings for access, allowances, and sign-out. Lab members can work privately under Personal. The navigation includes Instructor Console when your account has permission to use it. You can find shared class folders below the Session Rack.

Open a Recording

The Session Rack lists your newest splits first. When you open a recording, its mixer appears and the other recordings collapse to title rows. Their buttons remain available. Each status badge shows whether the recording is queued, processing, ready, or failed. When it reads READY, you can listen. A failed split does not count toward your daily allowance.

To rename your recording, you can click its title in the Session Rack, type a new title, and press Enter to save or Esc to cancel. You can also open the three-dot Split actions menu and choose Rename. That menu includes Export, + Folder, and public sharing controls. See Export & Sharing for the download contents and sharing permissions.

Mixer Controls

At the top of the mixer, you can play or pause, move through the recording, see the time, and add a note. You can also change the speed without changing the pitch. Each stem has a colored label, a waveform showing the sound over time, Solo and Mute buttons, and a download arrow. Solo cycles through Front, Only, and the previous mix. See The Mixer for details.

The vertical line marks your place in the recording. Click a waveform or a note's timestamp to move to a passage. Use Refresh class notes to reload classmates' notes. Below them, click the Listening Guy header to open its guide and chat. See The Mixer for controls and keyboard shortcuts.

Class Folders

Class Folders lists folders instructors have shared with your courses. You can expand a folder to see its recordings, then choose Load all to open them in the Session Rack. The app checks membership before access, and a folder disappears when you leave its course. On a recording assigned to a course, Class workspace opens that course.

Resplit a Stem

At the end of a stem's row, you can choose the curved-arrow Resplit button to separate that audio again. Choose the number of stems and where to keep the result, then choose Resplit. Separate split creates another entry. For your own saved recording in the same workspace, With original split groups the result beneath it. Check the remaining allowance shown in the dialog before starting.

Delete or Hide

Open Split actions to find Delete for your own recording. For a recording owned by someone else, Hide removes it from your rack until your next visit. Download any stems you need before deleting your recording. Once the app accepts your request, it hides the split and blocks access. It then removes private content after any active processing finishes, while preserving audio still used by another split.

Help and Credits

The footer identifies the separation model. Authorized instructors can open Instructor Console from the navigation. To report a problem, choose Report a bug. Use CUNY Login, or choose No account? and enter your first name, last name, and a .edu email address. Reports include your description, the page, and the app release, but do not automatically attach audio, notes, or conversations. See Privacy & Limits for report storage and delivery.
