Find Your Way Around

Use Splitter to import audio, choose a split, and open recordings in the Session Rack. Class Folders sit below the rack. In Class Workspace, choose a course to see its roster, usage, and folders. Students can open courses they belong to; instructors can open courses they teach. Settings shows daily allowances, links to the Lab, and sign-out. Instructors can also open the Instructor Console there to edit teaching instructions.

Open a Recording

The Session Rack lists newest splits first. Open one to expand its mixer; other recordings collapse to title rows with their actions still available. Status badges show queued, processing, ready, or failed. When a split is READY, you can listen. A failed split releases its place in the daily allowance.

Use Rename to change a title in both the rack and shared folders without changing the audio. Export downloads stems; see Export & Sharing for details. On course splits, + Folder adds the recording to a course folder. Copy link makes a public audio page, and Stop sharing revokes it.

Read the Mixer

The transport holds play and pause, elapsed and total time, the scrub bar, and + Note. Speed controls offer 0.5×, 0.75×, and 1× without changing pitch. Each stem row has a colored label, waveform, Solo, Mute, and a download arrow. A soloed stem's button reads Only.

The vertical playhead marks the current time. Click a waveform or a note's timestamp to jump to a passage. Use Refresh class notes to reload classmates' notes. Below them, click the Listening Guy header to open its guide and chat. See The Mixer for controls and keyboard shortcuts.

Load Class Folders

Class Folders lists folders shared by instructors with your courses. Expand a folder to inspect recordings or press Load all to put them in the rack. Membership is checked before access, and a folder disappears when you leave its course. Inside a course split, Class workspace opens the course that owns it.

Delete a Split

Download what you need first. Choosing Delete hides the split and blocks access after the server accepts the request. Cleanup removes private content once active processing settles, preserving audio still used by another run.

Use the Footer

The footer credits Demucs and links instructors to the Instructor Console. To report a problem, choose Report a bug and use CUNY Login, or select No account? and enter your first name, last name, and a .edu email address. The form includes page and release information but does not automatically attach audio, notes, or conversations. See Privacy & Limits for report storage and delivery.
