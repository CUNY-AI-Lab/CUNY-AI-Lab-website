Find Your Way Around

Use Splitter to import and split audio, then open recordings in the Session Rack. You can find Class Folders below the rack. In Class Workspace, choose a course to see its roster, usage, and folders. Students can open courses they belong to; instructors can open courses they teach. Settings shows daily allowances, links to the Lab, and sign-out. Instructors can also open the Instructor Console there to edit teaching instructions.

Open a Recording

The Session Rack lists your newest splits first. Open a recording to see its mixer; the others collapse to title rows, where their actions remain available. Each status badge shows whether the recording is queued, processing, ready, or failed. When it says READY, you can listen. A failed split does not count toward your daily allowance.

Use Rename to change a split's title in the rack and shared folders without changing the audio. Export downloads stems; see Export & Sharing for details. For a recording assigned to a course, + Folder adds it to a course folder. Copy link makes a public audio page, and Stop sharing revokes it.

Read the Mixer

At the top of the mixer, you can play or pause, move through the recording, see the time, and add a note. You can also change the speed without changing the pitch. Each stem has a colored label, a waveform showing the sound over time, Solo and Mute buttons, and a download arrow. When you solo a stem, its button reads Only.

The vertical line marks your place in the recording. Click a waveform or a note's timestamp to jump to a passage. Use Refresh class notes to reload classmates' notes. Below them, click the Listening Guy header to open its guide and chat. See The Mixer for controls and keyboard shortcuts.

Load Class Folders

Class Folders lists folders shared by instructors with your courses. Expand a folder to inspect recordings or press Load all to put them in the rack. Membership is checked before access, and a folder disappears when you leave its course. On a recording assigned to a course, Class workspace opens that course.

Delete a Split

Download anything you want to keep before choosing Delete. Once the app accepts your request, it hides the split and blocks access. It then removes private content after any active processing finishes, while preserving audio still used by another split.

Use the Footer

The footer credits Demucs and links instructors to the Instructor Console. To report a problem, choose Report a bug and use CUNY Login, or select No account? and enter your first name, last name, and a .edu email address. The form includes page and release information but does not automatically attach audio, notes, or conversations. See Privacy & Limits for report storage and delivery.
