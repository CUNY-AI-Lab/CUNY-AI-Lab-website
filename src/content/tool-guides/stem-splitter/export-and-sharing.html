Download Stems

To download all the stems from a recording as MP3 files, press Export in the Session Rack. To download one stem, use the arrow at the end of its row in the mixer. The app does not add a watermark or course information; use the recording within the terms of its license.

Make a Public Link

Choose Copy link on a finished split to make a public listening page. Anyone with the link can play and download the stems, change the mix, and move through the recording without signing in. Stop sharing immediately turns the link off, but it cannot remove files someone has already downloaded.

The public page excludes notes, custom labels, Listening Guy content, the original upload, and your identity. Its audio link expires at the end of the split's 90-day lifetime.

Share with a Class

On the Splitter, use Share with to choose whether new work belongs to Personal or a course. When you acknowledge the course policy, your instructor can review that work and its Listening Guy conversations. Classmates gain access through a separate folder permission.

Instructors create folders in Class Workspace and add course recordings with + Folder in the rack. A folder starts with Instructors only access. To let current class members listen, choose Class: listen and read notes or Class: listen and add notes, then press Save sharing. Original uploads and Listening Guy conversations remain outside the shared view.

Review the Course Record

Instructors can open Course record in Class Workspace to review assigned splits and usage. It covers only work the app still retains.
