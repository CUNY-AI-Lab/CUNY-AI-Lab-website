Download Stems

In the Session Rack, you can download all the stems from a recording as MP3 files by choosing Export. To download one stem, choose the arrow at the end of its row in the mixer. The app does not add a watermark or course information. Use the recording within the terms of its license.

Public Links

In the Session Rack, you can choose Copy link on a finished split to create a public listening page, then share the link. Anyone with the link can play and download the stems, change the mix, and move through the recording without signing in. Stop sharing immediately turns the link off, but it cannot remove files someone has already downloaded.

The public page excludes notes, custom labels, Listening Guy content, the original upload, and your identity. The audio link expires 90 days after the split.

Class Sharing

On the Splitter tab, use Share with to choose whether new work belongs to Personal or a course. When you check the acknowledgment, your instructor can review that work and its Listening Guy conversations. Classmates gain access through a separate folder permission.

Instructors create folders in Class Workspace and add course recordings with + Folder in the Session Rack. A folder starts with Instructors only access. For class listening, choose Class: listen and read notes or Class: listen and add notes, then choose Save sharing. Original uploads and Listening Guy conversations remain outside the shared view.

Course Record

Instructors can open Course record in Class Workspace to review assigned splits and usage. The record includes only work that is still available in the app.
