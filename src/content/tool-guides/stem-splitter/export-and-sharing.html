Download Stems

Press Export on a rack entry to download its stems as MP3 files. For one stem, use the download arrow at the end of its row. The app adds no watermark or course metadata. Use the recording within its license terms.

Make a Public Link

Choose Copy link on a finished split. Anyone with the link can play the stems, change the mix, seek, and download audio without signing in. Use Stop sharing to revoke the link immediately; it cannot recall files someone has already downloaded.

The public page excludes notes, custom labels, Listening Guy content, the original upload, and your identity. Its audio link expires at the end of the split's 90-day lifetime.

Share with a Class

Use Share with on the Splitter to assign new work to Personal or a course. The course acknowledgment permits instructor review of that new work and its Listening Guy conversations. Classmate access is a separate folder permission.

Instructors create folders in Class Workspace and add course recordings with + Folder in the rack. A folder starts with Instructors only access. To let current class members listen, choose Class: listen and read notes or Class: listen and add notes, then press Save sharing. Original uploads and Listening Guy conversations remain outside the shared view.

Review the Course Record

Instructors can open Course record in Class Workspace to review assigned splits and usage. It covers only work the app still retains.
