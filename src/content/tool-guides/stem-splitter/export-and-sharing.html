Download Stems

In the Session Rack, open the recording's three-dot Split actions menu and choose Export under Recording. The ZIP includes MP3 stems and a text file with the available listening guide, conversation, and notes. Source credits are included for supported imports. Review those files before sharing them. To download just one stem, choose the downward arrow at the end of its row in the mixer.

Public Links

In the Session Rack, open Split actions and choose Copy link on a finished split to create a public listening page, then share the link. Anyone with the link can play and download the stems, change the mix, and move through the recording without signing in. Stop sharing immediately turns the link off, but it cannot remove files someone has already downloaded.

The public page excludes notes, custom labels, Listening Guy content, the original upload, and your identity. The audio link expires 90 days after the split.

Class Sharing

On the Splitter tab, use Share with to choose whether new work belongs to Personal or a course. When you check the acknowledgment, your instructor can review that work and its Listening Guy conversations. Classmates gain access through a separate folder permission.

Instructors create folders on the Classes page and add course recordings with Add to class folder in Split actions. A new folder stays Instructors only until you choose who can open it. For class listening, choose Class: listen and read notes or Class: listen and add notes, then choose Save sharing. Original uploads and Listening Guy conversations remain outside the shared view.

Class Roster

Instructors can open Students on the Classes page to review assigned splits and usage. The counts include only splits still within the 90-day retention period.
