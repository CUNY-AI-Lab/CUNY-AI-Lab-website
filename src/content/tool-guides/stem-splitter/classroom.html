Assign New Work to a Course

On the Splitter tab, choose a course under Share with, then check the acknowledgment allowing your instructor to review new splits and their Listening Guy conversations. Classmates gain access only when an instructor shares a course folder.

Share with starts at Personal, even if you arrive through an instructor's ?course= link. Once you acknowledge the policy, each new split belongs to the selected course. Changing courses clears the checkbox, so acknowledge the new course before continuing. Existing personal work stays personal.

Open the Workspace

Use the Class selector to choose a course you belong to or teach. Press Create a split to return to the Splitter, where Share with starts at Personal; choose the course and acknowledge its policy for new course work. Instructors can open Class settings to change options such as shared stem labels, or Course record to review assigned splits still within retention. The workspace updates while open; use Refresh to reload the roster and folders after a recent change.

Review the Roster

Instructors see every enrolled student, including those with no splits. Each row lists assigned splits, statuses, dates, and a link to the student's Listening Guy conversation. Counts cover only loaded roster entries; load further pages before treating a count as the course total. Students who have not opened the app show App usage pending.

Enrollment comes from the Lab's admission records, and the app checks membership on every protected request. A withdrawn student loses course access. A copied folder link does not grant membership or access.

Create and Share a Folder

As an instructor, create a folder in the Folders card, then use + Folder on a course split in the rack to add it. Folders accept only splits assigned to their course and start with Instructors only access.

Choose a permission and press Save sharing. Keep Instructors only to limit the folder to course instructors. Choose Class: listen and read notes so current class members can play stems and read notes, or Class: listen and add notes when they need to annotate.

Share the folder link or direct students to Class Folders on the Splitter tab. Viewers must currently belong to the course. They see only the folder's splits, not original uploads or Listening Guy conversations. Revoking sharing hides the folder immediately. Expired splits remain as placeholders; folders do not extend retention.

Read Course Conversations

From the roster, instructors can open a student's course split and Listening Guy sessions. Review costs no turn, generates no answer, and allows neither edits nor forwarding. Personal splits and conversations from before the student acknowledged the course policy stay private.
