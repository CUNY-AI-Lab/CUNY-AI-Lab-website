Course Work

On the Splitter tab, you can choose a course under Share with. Before creating work for that course, check the acknowledgment allowing your instructor to review new splits and their Listening Guy conversations. Classmates gain access only when an instructor shares a class folder.

When you open Stem Splitter directly, Share with defaults to Personal. If you open it from a class link, the app checks your access and selects that class. Once you check the acknowledgment, new splits belong to the selected course. If you change courses, check the acknowledgment for the new course before continuing. Work you already created under Personal stays there.

The Classes Page

Open Classes and choose a course from the Class selector. Folders lists the course's folders, My splits lists your finished splits in the course, and Students shows the class roster and usage. Under My splits, New class split opens the Splitter tab with that class selected under Share with. Check the acknowledgment before creating work for the course. Class settings opens the course's Listening Guy instructions under Account. The page updates automatically while it is open, and Refresh reloads it.

Each split under My splits offers Open split, Annotations, and Listening Guy messages. Choose a class folder beside a split, then choose Add to folder to file it there.

Roster and Activity

Under Students, Class roster and usage counts enrolled students, assigned splits, and splits still within the 90-day retention period. Open a student to see their course splits, the notes under Course annotations, and their saved Listening Guy session. Counts include only the students currently loaded, so choose Load more students before using a count for the whole course. A demo course shows a Sample roster instead.

Under Account → Settings, choose a course under Instructions for to see its Class roster, which lists class assignments and recorded app activity. Visit and play counts cover the past 30 days, with collection beginning October 29, 2026.

The course roster comes from the Lab's admission records. Students who leave a course lose access, and a folder link does not grant access to someone who is not a member.

Class Folders

As an instructor, you can create a folder under Folders on the Classes page. Type a name under New folder and choose Create folder. Only instructors see a new folder until you choose who can open it. To add one of your recordings, open Split actions on it in the Session Rack and choose Add to class folder, or use Add to folder under My splits. To add a student's split, open the student under Students. The recording must be assigned to that course.

Open a folder, choose an option under Who can use this folder?, then choose Save sharing. Instructors only limits the folder to course instructors. With Class: listen and read notes, current class members can play stems and read notes. With Class: listen and add notes, they can also add notes.

Under Class folder link, choose Copy link to share the folder, or direct students to Folders on the Classes page. Only current class members can open the folder. They can see the splits in it, but not the original uploads or Listening Guy conversations. If you stop sharing the folder, it is hidden immediately. Expired splits remain listed, but their audio is no longer available.

Course Conversations

Under Students, instructors can open a student's course split and read their Listening Guy conversations. Reading a conversation does not use a turn or generate an answer, and instructors cannot edit or forward it. Personal splits and conversations from before the student checked the acknowledgment remain private.
