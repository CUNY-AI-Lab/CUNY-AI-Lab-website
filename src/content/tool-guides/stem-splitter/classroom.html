Assign New Work to a Course

On the Splitter tab, choose a course under Share with, then check the acknowledgment allowing your instructor to review new splits and their Listening Guy conversations. Classmates gain access only when an instructor shares a class folder.

When you open Stem Splitter directly, Share with defaults to Personal. If you open it from a class link, the app checks your access and selects that class. Once you check the acknowledgment, new splits belong to the selected course. If you change courses, check the acknowledgment for the new course before continuing. Work you already created under Personal stays there.

Open the Workspace

Choose a course from the Class selector, then choose Create a split to open Stem Splitter with that class selected under Share with. Check the acknowledgment before creating work for the course. If your access has changed or the access check fails, choose a workspace before creating a split. Instructors can change labels in Class settings and review work the app still retains in Course record. The workspace updates while it is open, and Refresh reloads the roster and folders.

Review the Roster

The roster shows every enrolled student, including those who have not created a split. Each row lists their course splits, their status and dates, and a link to their Listening Guy conversation. Counts include only the students currently loaded, so load the remaining pages before using a count for the whole course. Students who have not opened the app appear as App usage pending.

In Settings, instructors can also open Student activity to review recorded visits and plays alongside the number of recordings a student has split. Listening to an existing recording can appear there. Visit and play counts cover the past 30 days, with collection beginning October 29, 2026.

The course roster comes from the Lab's admission records. Students who leave a course lose access, and a folder link does not grant access to someone who is not a member.

Create and Share a Folder

As an instructor, create a folder in the Folders card, then choose + Folder on a recording in the Session Rack to add it. The recording must be assigned to that course. Folders start with Instructors only access.

Select a permission, then choose Save sharing. Instructors only limits the folder to course instructors. With Class: listen and read notes, current class members can play stems and read notes. With Class: listen and add notes, they can also add notes.

Share the folder link or direct students to Class Folders on the Splitter tab. Students must belong to the course to open the folder. They can see the splits in it, but not the original uploads or Listening Guy conversations. If you stop sharing the folder, it is hidden immediately. Expired splits remain listed, but their audio is no longer available.

Read Course Conversations

From the roster, instructors can open a student's course split and read their Listening Guy conversations. Reading a conversation does not use a turn or generate an answer, and instructors cannot edit or forward it. Personal splits and conversations from before the student checked the acknowledgment remain private.

The app deletes uploaded audio and stems 90 days after a split. Upload only music you are entitled to use for class.
