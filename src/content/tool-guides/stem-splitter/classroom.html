Assign New Work to a Course

On the Splitter tab, choose a course under Share with, then check the acknowledgment allowing your instructor to review new splits and their Listening Guy conversations. Classmates gain access only when an instructor shares a course folder.

When you open the Splitter directly, Share with defaults to Personal. If you open it from a class link, the app checks your access and selects that class. Once you acknowledge the policy, new splits belong to the selected course. If you change courses, check the acknowledgment for the new course before continuing. Work you already created in Personal stays there.

Open the Workspace

Choose a course from the Class selector, then press Create a split to open the Splitter with that class selected under Share with. Check the acknowledgment before creating work for the course. If your access has changed or the access check fails, choose a workspace before creating a split. Instructors can change labels in Class settings and review work the app still retains in Course record. The workspace updates while it is open; use Refresh to reload the roster and folders after a recent change.

Review the Roster

The roster shows every enrolled student, including those who have not created a split. Each row lists their course splits, their status and dates, and a link to their Listening Guy conversation. Counts include only the students currently loaded, so load the remaining pages before using a count for the whole course. Students who have not opened the app appear as App usage pending.

In Settings, instructors can also open Student activity to review recorded visits and plays alongside the number of recordings a student has split. Listening to an existing recording can appear there. Visit and play counts cover the past 30 days, with collection beginning October 29, 2026.

Enrollment comes from the Lab's admission records, and the app checks membership on every protected request. A withdrawn student loses course access. A copied folder link does not grant membership or access.

Create and Share a Folder

As an instructor, create a folder in the Folders card, then use + Folder on a recording in the rack to add it. The recording must be assigned to that course. Folders start with Instructors only access.

Choose a permission and press Save sharing. Keep Instructors only to limit the folder to course instructors. Choose Class: listen and read notes so current class members can play stems and read notes, or Class: listen and add notes when they need to annotate.

Share the folder link or direct students to Class Folders on the Splitter tab. Viewers must currently belong to the course. They see only the folder's splits, not original uploads or Listening Guy conversations. Revoking sharing hides the folder immediately. Expired splits remain as placeholders; folders do not extend retention.

Read Course Conversations

From the roster, instructors can open a student's course split and read their Listening Guy conversations. Reading a conversation does not use a turn or generate an answer, and instructors cannot edit or forward it. Personal splits and conversations from before the student acknowledged the course policy remain private.
