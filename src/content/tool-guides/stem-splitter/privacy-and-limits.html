Choose a course under Share with and check its acknowledgment to assign new work there. Earlier work stays personal. Instructors can read course conversations without generating answers, editing, or forwarding them. Classmates need current membership and an instructor's folder grant. To allow annotation, choose Class: listen and add notes, then Save sharing.

Retention and Deletion

Uploaded audio and stems are deleted 90 days after the split, when public listening links also expire. Shared folders retain an expired split as a placeholder, not its audio; a folder does not extend retention.

Download any stems you need before deleting a split or reaching its retention limit. Choosing Delete hides the split and blocks further access after the server accepts the request. Cleanup removes private content once active processing settles, preserving audio still used by another run.

Daily Allowances

For signed-in members, Settings shows remaining split runs, Listening Guy turns, and the reset time. Both reset at midnight UTC: 8:00 PM Eastern during daylight time and 7:00 PM during standard time. Successful splits count; failed or cancelled splits release their places. Reading generated guides and existing conversations costs no turn. Settings also estimates the separate model allowance shared across CUNY AI Lab tools.

Sources and Licensing

Uploads can be MP3, WAV, FLAC, M4A, OGG, or AIFF files up to 100 MB. Use music you are entitled to use for class. Internet Archive search returns public, downloadable MP3s with music metadata and a credited source. The app's duration limit gives verified students and instructors more time than visitors. Metadata alone does not establish reuse permission.

Pasting a YouTube link looks up the video's title and offers an Internet Archive search. This interface does not retrieve YouTube audio, even if you sign in to YouTube.

The Audio source selector also offers Wikimedia Commons. Supported recordings need verified public-domain, CC0, or CC BY rights. The app preserves their creator, source page, license, and credit, including attribution in exports. A composition's age does not establish a recording's rights. ccMixter is unavailable.

Guest Access

At the October 29, 2026 check, the app's public runtime settings advertised guest splitting and Listening Guy as available. The guest allowance is five successful splits per UTC day. Guest work stays private to that browser and follows the same retention rules. After signing in, visitors can claim their work into an account. Course features require a signed-in member.

The implemented guest Listening Guy demo allows two successful replies for the entire browser session. Cue and chat share that total across recordings, tabs, reloads, resets, and UTC days. Failed, empty, cancelled, or incomplete replies release their places. The third request asks the visitor to sign in before generating another reply. The October 29 check read only the advertised configuration; it did not test session creation or a successful model reply.

Report a Problem

Open Report a bug in the footer and describe the problem. Use CUNY Login, or choose No account? and enter your first name, last name, and a .edu email address. Signed-in identity is verified; guest contact details are self-reported and grant no app access. Reports include your description, the page, and app release, but do not automatically attach audio, notes, or conversations.

Reports and delivery records are stored privately for support. Redmine is preferred, with email fallback if Redmine is unconfigured or has definitely rejected the report. Uncertain delivery stays pending to avoid duplicates. “Email accepted for delivery” means the mail service accepted it, not that it reached a mailbox. For access questions, email ailab@gc.cuny.edu.
