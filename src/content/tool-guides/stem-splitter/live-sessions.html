Before You Start

When students open a shared recording, they usually control playback themselves. During a live session, they can follow the instructor's playback and listen to the selected stems on their own devices.

Ask students to bring headphones, and project your mixer if you want them to see which stems they hear.

Lead the Session

As a course instructor or authorized guest instructor, open a recording assigned to the course and press Start shared session above the playback controls. Play, pause, move to a passage, change speed, or solo a stem as you teach. Students who follow will receive those changes. You cannot start a shared session from Personal or a public link.

Keep your tab visible. Hiding it pauses playback; press Resume shared session when you return. Your control of the session is reserved for 90 seconds at a time and renews while you lead, so another instructor cannot take over while the reservation is active. Finish with End shared session to return followers to independent listening.

Follow the Instructor

Open the same recording from the class folder and press Follow instructor. If playback is blocked, press Join shared audio to give the browser the initial click it requires.

Your mute switches still affect what you hear, even when the instructor plays every stem. Choose Leave shared session for independent control. If the connection drops, press Retry session connection, then Follow instructor again.

Allow for the Delay

Students may take a few seconds to catch up when you move to a different point in the recording. Allow time for everyone to reach the passage before discussing it.

If a student hides the tab, their audio pauses. When they return, they can use Join shared audio to resume. Course access and note permissions still apply, and Listening Guy conversations are not part of the shared session.
