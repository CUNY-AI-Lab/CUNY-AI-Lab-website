Before You Start

When students open a shared recording, they usually control playback themselves. During a live session, they can follow the instructor's playback and listen to the selected stems on their own devices.

Ask students to bring headphones, and project your mixer if you want them to see which stems are playing.

Leading a Session

As a course instructor or authorized guest instructor, you can open a course recording in the Session Rack, then choose Start shared session above the playback controls. Play, pause, move to a passage, change speed, or solo a stem as you teach. Students who follow will receive those changes. You cannot start a shared session from Personal or a public link.

Leave your tab visible. Hiding it pauses playback, so choose Resume shared session when you return. The app reserves control for you in 90-second intervals and renews it while you lead, preventing another instructor from taking over during that time. Finish with End shared session to return followers to independent listening.

Following a Session

To join, you can open the same recording from the class folder and choose Follow instructor. If playback is blocked, choose Join shared audio, since the browser requires an initial click.

Your mute switches still control which stems play on your device, even when the instructor plays every stem. Choose Leave shared session for independent control. If the connection drops, choose Retry session connection, then Follow instructor again.

Playback Delays

Students may take a few seconds to catch up when you move to a different point in the recording. Allow time for everyone to reach the passage before discussing it.

If a student hides the tab, their audio pauses. When they return, they can choose Join shared audio to resume. Course access and note permissions still apply, and Listening Guy conversations are not part of the shared session.
