Before You Start

Students normally control playback themselves when they open a shared recording. In a live session, students can follow the instructor's playback and hear the selected stems on their own devices.

Ask students to bring headphones, and project your mixer if you want them to see which stems they hear.

Lead the Session

As a course instructor or authorized guest instructor, open a recording assigned to the course and press Start shared session above the playback controls. Play, pause, move to a passage, change speed, or solo a stem as you teach. Students who follow will receive those changes. You cannot start a shared session from Personal or a public link.

Keep your tab visible. Hiding it pauses playback; press Resume shared session when you return. Control is reserved for 90 seconds at a time and renews while you lead, so another instructor cannot take over during an active reservation. Finish with End shared session to return followers to independent listening.

Follow the Instructor

Open the same recording from the class folder and press Follow instructor. If playback is blocked, press Join shared audio to give the browser the initial click it requires.

Your mute switches still affect what you hear, even when the instructor plays every stem. Choose Leave shared session for independent control. If the connection drops, press Retry session connection, then Follow instructor again.

Allow for the Delay

Followers check for cues periodically and may take a few seconds to reach your position. Pause before asking about a passage; for a question at 1:10, wait until everyone reaches that time before playing again.

A hidden follower tab pauses its audio. On returning, students can use Join shared audio to resume. Course access and note permissions still apply, and Listening Guy conversations are not part of the shared session.
