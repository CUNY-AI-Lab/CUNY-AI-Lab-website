Instruction Scope

After signing in, you can open Instructor Console from the navigation if your account has instructor access. To edit teaching instructions for a course, select that course in Workspace and choose Class settings. Check the course name before saving. Personal and guest work use the default Listening Guy instructions.

Teaching Instructions

In Teaching instructions, tell Listening Guy what you want students to work on and how you would like it to respond. You can mention a concept or your students' experience with music. You can write up to 2,000 characters. The app adds your instructions to its fixed system prompt for each guide and answer, and you cannot edit the fixed prompt.

Add the required change note, up to 240 characters, explaining what changed and why, then save. New answers use the instruction immediately. After you change the instructions, the app generates a new guide the next time someone requests it. To reset the instruction, clear the field and save with a change note. Use Preview saved prompt to see the assembled prompt sent to the model. Under Prompt reference, you can see each saved revision and its change note.

Teaching Examples

The demo course introduces monophony, or one melody, and polyphony, or different melodies sounding together. Students can listen to the recording to consider how the parts relate. Listening Guy does not receive the recording or its waveforms, so students need to listen to check its descriptions.

You might ask Listening Guy to explain a concept in language your students can use, or to say when it is unsure. Read the saved course conversations to see how students are using it.

Search Examples

In the app-wide Instructor Console, you can expand Search examples to choose the songs suggested in the search field. Use Find a song to add a recording, then reorder the list with Move up and Move down. You can also change how long each title stays visible and whether the list shuffles after each pass. Choose Save songs to apply your changes. Course-specific teaching instructions are edited separately in the course console.
