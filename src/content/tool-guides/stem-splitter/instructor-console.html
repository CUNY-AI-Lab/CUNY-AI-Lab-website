Check the Scope

Open the Instructor Console from Settings or the footer link. Before editing, check the scope notice. A course Console affects that course's work, whereas the application-wide Console affects non-course work. Neither setting is private to your instructor account. Each scope has its own revision history.

Save a Teaching Instruction

In Teaching instructions, tell Listening Guy what you want students to work on and how you would like it to respond. You can mention a concept or your students' experience with music. You can write up to 2,000 characters. The app adds your instructions to its fixed system prompt for each guide and answer, and you cannot edit the fixed prompt.

Add the required change note, up to 240 characters, explaining what changed and why, then save. New answers use the instruction immediately. An older guide regenerates when next requested after a prompt change. To reset the instruction, clear the field and save with a change note. Use Preview saved prompt to see the assembled prompt sent to the model. Under Prompt reference, you can see each saved revision and its change note.

Connect a Concept to Listening

The demo course introduces monophony, or one melody, and polyphony, or different melodies sounding together. Students can listen to the recording to consider how the parts relate. Listening Guy does not receive the recording or its waveforms, so students need to listen to check its descriptions.

You might ask Listening Guy to explain a concept in language your students can use, or to say when it is unsure. Read the saved course conversations to see how students are using it.

Choose Search Examples

Use Search examples to set the songs suggested beneath the Internet Archive search field on the Splitter tab in Stem Splitter. Check each recording's audio quality and your permission to use it before suggesting it to students.

Stem Splitter grew from the Critical AI Literacy Institute and continues with the CUNY AI Lab.

The app deletes uploaded audio and stems 90 days after a split. Upload only music you are entitled to use for class.
