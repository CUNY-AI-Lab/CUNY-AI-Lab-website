About Stem Splitter

Stem Splitter helps you listen closely to a recording, even if you do not have a background in music. It separates the recording into parts called stems, such as vocals, drums, and bass, so you can hear each one on its own or play them together. You can slow down a passage, mark a moment with a note, and use Listening Guy to ask questions about what you hear.

Ethnomusicology professor Agustina Checa created Stem Splitter for music teaching. It was developed through the Critical AI Literacy Institute, and the CUNY AI Lab hosts it.

Listening Guy can suggest questions and operate the mixer. Listening Guy does not receive the recording or its waveforms, so listen to check its descriptions.
