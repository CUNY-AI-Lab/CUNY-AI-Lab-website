Read the Opening Guide

When a split is ready, Listening Guy writes a guide about the recording, its genre, and possible listening questions. The app saves it for the song and shares it across the class. Reading the guide costs no turn.

Ask and Try an Action

Type a question about an instrument, section, or unfamiliar term into ask about this song… and press Send. Listening Guy can read pinned notes and, when its answer calls for an action, mute or solo stems, move the playhead, or pin a note. Transcript chips such as MUTE · BASS show what changed. Press Esc to clear solos and the active loop.

The line above the input shows how many turns remain today. Failed replies cost no turn, and retrying an interrupted reply does not charge a second turn. If the provider is unavailable, the panel explains the problem; the mixer remains usable.

Press Reset to clear the saved conversation for this split and start again. Earlier session restores history when you return, but does not preserve conversations you reset. Reset does not restore spent turns.

Check the Claim

Listening Guy works from the title and artist, stem layout, notes, and conversation. It has no direct evidence from the audio, so a description of a backbeat may reflect general genre knowledge rather than this performance.

In the demonstration, the model muted the requested stems but acknowledged that it could not identify an exact moment. Students had to find the passage themselves. Follow Ask, Then Verify: choose a claim, try a mixer action, and pin a note describing what you actually hear.
