Read the Opening Guide

When a split is ready, Listening Guy can generate a guide about the recording and suggest questions. The app can save the guide with the recording. Reading an existing guide does not use a turn.

Ask and Try an Action

Type a question about an instrument, section, or unfamiliar term into ask about this song… and press Send. Listening Guy can read pinned notes and, when its answer calls for an action, mute or solo stems, move the playhead, or pin a note. The conversation shows any actions it takes. Press Esc to clear solos and the active loop.

The line above the input shows how many turns remain today. Failed replies cost no turn, and retrying an interrupted reply does not charge a second turn. Guide requests may fail. If a request fails, the app preserves your question and shows available retry guidance and support details. The mixer remains usable.

Press Reset to clear the saved conversation for this split and start again. Earlier session restores history when you return, but does not preserve conversations you reset. Reset does not restore spent turns.

Check the Claim

Listening Guy uses the recording's title, the names and labels of the stems, its duration, notes, any instructor instructions, and the conversation. It does not receive the audio or the waveforms shown in the mixer. Listen to the recording to check its descriptions.

In the demonstration, Listening Guy muted the requested stems but could not identify an exact moment. Students listened to find the passage themselves. Try Ask, Then Verify and pin a note about what you hear.

Plan Your Turns

At the October 29, 2026 check, the app's public runtime settings advertised guest splitting and the Listening Guy demo as available. This was a configuration check, not a test of a successful model reply. See Privacy & Limits for the two-reply guest limit and how it applies.

Signed-in members receive a daily allowance of successful turns. Check Settings or the chat panel for the remaining count. It resets at midnight UTC, which is 8:00 PM Eastern during daylight time and 7:00 PM during standard time. Reading generated guides and existing conversations costs no turn.

Guide and Review Course Work

Instructors can set vocabulary and listening tasks in the Instructor Console. Check the scope before saving. Course instructions affect course work, and application-wide instructions affect non-course work.

Use Class Workspace to read conversations attached to your course's splits. Students acknowledge this access before assigning work to the course. Review costs no turn and allows no edits.
