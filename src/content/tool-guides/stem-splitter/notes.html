Add and Revisit a Note

Move the playhead to the passage and press + Note in the transport row. You can pause first or add a note during playback. Type your comment and save; it appears beneath the stems with your name and the timestamp. Click a timestamp to return to that passage, or use [ and ] for the previous or next note.

Check Who Has Access

Notes on personal splits stay private to your account. Course splits also give the instructor access. Current class members can read notes in a folder shared with the class. To allow annotation, an instructor chooses Class: listen and add notes and presses Save sharing. The course acknowledgment covers instructor review; the folder permission controls class access.

Course notes show the author's CUNY Login-verified name, and only that author can edit or delete them. A note added by Listening Guy records the requesting student's account as its initiator.

Keep Shared Notes Current

Notes on an open shared split refresh every few seconds without disturbing playback or your draft. Use Refresh class notes for an immediate reload. If a connection problem prevents saving, your draft stays in the box for another attempt.

Give the Next Listener Something to Check

Name a layer and an audible change, such as “Bass drops out here.” You can also suggest an action: the demonstration's note asks students to hear vocals and drums alone, then compare vocals with another stem.

For a section boundary, label the verse, chorus, bridge, or solo and describe the sound marking the change. Classmates can revisit the passage and explain whether they agree. Listening Guy can read these notes when answering later questions.
