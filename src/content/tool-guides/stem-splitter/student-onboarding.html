Prepare the First Session

Send the course invitation from the Lab portal and keep its invitation window open through add/drop. Prepare a Week 1 folder with two or three recordings you have split and checked. Choose Class: listen and add notes, then Save sharing, so current class members can add notes. Post links to the app and this documentation on your course site.

Listen Together on Day One

Allow about fifteen minutes for sign-in, including multi-factor prompts. Students open the app from My classes with CUNY Login, find Week 1 under Class Folders, and press Load all to open its recordings in the rack.

Choose a recording everyone can open and give students time to listen to its different parts. They can try solo and mute, then pin a note at a moment they want to return to. Check that everyone can save a note before the session ends.

Build on the First Listen

The Sample Activities include ideas for listening and taking notes with a prepared recording. Students can also choose Personal to practice splitting a recording outside the course. When you introduce Listening Guy, let them know that it does not receive the recording or its waveforms, and that they can check its descriptions by listening.

Explain What Gets Shared

Before assigning new course work, students acknowledge that you can review their course splits and Listening Guy conversations. Classmates gain access only through an instructor-shared folder. Choose its listening or note-taking permission and press Save sharing. Existing personal splits stay private. Show students Share with and let them find Personal themselves. They can practice there before creating work for the course.

Work Through Common Problems

If someone cannot see the course, check the roster in Class Workspace. They may still need to accept an invitation, or its window may have closed. If playback is silent, ask them to press play. In live sessions, they can press Join shared audio.

For a failed split, check the file format and 100 MB limit, then retry or use Archive search. The provider may be busy. A failed split does not count toward the daily allowance.

If Listening Guy cannot answer, check remaining turns and the panel's message. During provider outages, the mixer and notes remain available. For other problems, use Report a bug in the footer. Students can use CUNY Login or No account? with their first name, last name, and a .edu email address. The form includes page and release information without automatically attaching audio, notes, or conversations.
