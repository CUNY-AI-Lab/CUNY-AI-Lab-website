Prepare the First Session

Send the course invitation from the Lab portal and keep its invitation window open through add/drop. Prepare a Week 1 folder with two or three split recordings you have checked. Choose Class: listen and add notes, then Save sharing, so current class members can annotate. Post links to the app and this documentation on your course site.

Listen Together on Day One

Allow about fifteen minutes for sign-in, including multi-factor prompts. Students open the app from My classes with CUNY Login, find Week 1 under Class Folders, and press Load all to open its recordings in the rack.

Have everyone solo the bass on the same song for thirty seconds. Clear the solo, mute the bass, and replay the passage. Ask what changed, then have each student pin a note at a moment supporting their answer. Check that everyone can save a note before leaving.

Build on the First Listen

Assign One Stem at a Time from the prepared folder. Students can optionally practice importing under Personal; that uses one split run and keeps the recording outside the course. Later, introduce Ask, Then Verify. Explain that Listening Guy cannot hear audio, and ask students to check a guide's claim with the mixer before sending a follow-up.

Explain What Gets Shared

Before assigning new course work, students acknowledge that you can review their course splits and Listening Guy conversations. Classmates gain access only through an instructor-shared folder. Choose its listening or note-taking permission and press Save sharing. Existing personal splits stay private. Show students Share with and let them find Personal themselves. They can practice there before creating work for the course.

Work Through Common Problems

If someone cannot see the course, check the roster in Class Workspace. They may still need to accept an invitation, or its window may have closed. For silent playback, ask them to press play once. In live sessions, Join shared audio supplies the browser's required click.

For a failed split, check the file format and 100 MB limit, then retry or use Archive search. The provider may be busy. Failed splits release their places in the daily allowance.

If Listening Guy cannot answer, check remaining turns and the panel's message. During provider outages, the mixer and notes remain available. For other problems, use Report a bug in the footer. Students can use CUNY Login or No account? with their first name, last name, and a .edu email address. The form includes page and release information without automatically attaching audio, notes, or conversations.
