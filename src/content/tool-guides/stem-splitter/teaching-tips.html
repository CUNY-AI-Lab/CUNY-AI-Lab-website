Prepare the Recording

Split the recordings you want to use and organize them in shared folders. Check the source's license and audio quality before sharing. Use read-only access for demonstrations, or allow class notes when students will annotate.

You can rename a split in the Session Rack. Instructors can also change labels in Class settings. Listen to a recording before changing a label, since a stem may contain more than one sound.

Give Students Time to Listen

Students can begin with the full recording, then listen to individual stems to hear parts that may be harder to notice together. Slowing down a passage can help them follow a rhythm or phrase, and the waveforms can help them find places where the sound changes.

During a live session, you can project the mixer while students listen on their own devices. They can also work independently with recordings in a shared folder.

Use Notes and Questions

Students can pin notes to moments they want to remember or discuss. If the folder allows class notes, they can read one another's observations and return to those moments together.

Listening Guy can answer questions, but it does not receive the recording or its waveforms. Encourage students to listen for themselves when considering its descriptions. The Listening Guy guide describes the information it receives.
