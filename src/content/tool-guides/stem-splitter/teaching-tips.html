Prepare the Recording

Split the recordings you want to use and organize them in class folders. Check the source's license and audio quality before sharing. Use Class: listen and read notes for demonstrations, or Class: listen and add notes when students will annotate.

You can rename a split in the Session Rack. Instructors can also change labels in Class settings. Listen to a recording before changing a label, since a stem may contain more than its label suggests.

Allow Time to Listen

Students can begin with the full recording, then listen to individual stems to hear parts that may be harder to notice together. Slowing down a passage can help them follow a rhythm or phrase, and the waveforms can help them find places where the sound changes.

During a live session, you can project the mixer while students listen on their own devices. They can also work independently with recordings in a class folder.

Use Notes and Questions

Students can pin notes to moments they want to remember or discuss. If the folder allows class notes, they can read one another's notes and return to those moments together.

Listening Guy can answer questions. Listening Guy does not receive the recording or its waveforms, so students need to listen to check its descriptions. See Listening Guy for the information it receives.
