Prepare a recording and share it with the class so students can listen without using their daily allowance for splitting recordings. To let them add notes, choose Class: listen and add notes and press Save sharing. Check the daily Listening Guy allowance in Settings before planning a chat activity.

Listen to the Parts

Choose a recording students may not know and give them time to listen to it as a whole. They can then listen to individual stems, describe the sounds they hear, and leave notes at moments they want to discuss. A stem may include sounds that do not match its label.

Find Changes in the Music

Students can use the waveforms and playback controls to find places where the music changes, then add notes to mark what they hear. In a folder that allows class notes, they can read one another's notes and return to those passages together.

Ask Listening Guy

Students can ask Listening Guy about a musical term or a passage they have noticed, then return to the recording and their notes to consider its response. Listening Guy does not receive the recording or its waveforms, so its descriptions need to be checked by listening.

Listen Together

In a live session, an instructor can lead playback while students listen on their own devices. Students can also open a recording from a shared folder and listen independently without using their allowance for splitting recordings.
