Play and Move Through a Recording

Press the round play button or the space bar to play and pause all stems together. Drag the scrub handle or click a waveform to move to a passage. The left and right arrow keys move five seconds at a time.

Choose 0.5×, 0.75×, or 1× playback. Slower speeds preserve pitch, so a bass line stays in the same key. Slow a passage to count a rhythm or follow a phrase, then compare it at full speed.

Solo and Mute

Choose Mute to silence one stem. Solo silences the others and changes the chosen stem's button to Only. You can solo several stems, such as vocals and drums, to listen to them together.

During independent listening, your mix stays local to your browser. In a live session, the instructor sends an instrument focus, but your own mute switches still apply.

Repeat a Passage

Press L to loop the current region and listen again to a riff, cadence, or drum fill. Press Esc to clear both the loop and any solo.

Labels and Colors

otherHorns, strings, organ, synths, and other sounds outside the named stems.
guitarAcoustic and electric guitar. Available when you choose six stems.
pianoIncludes piano and some keyboard parts. Available when you choose six stems.

When you choose two stems, instrumental contains the accompaniment. Instructors can rename labels in Class settings. See Class Workspace for course settings.

Check What the Split Contains

The labels describe what the app tries to separate. The other stem may contain several instruments or room sound. Older recordings may leave hiss in vocals or little sound in drums.

Even when you choose six stems, some piano may appear in other or guitar may appear in another stem. Listen to the recording before naming an instrument. Sample Activities suggests ways to discuss what you hear.
