Play and Move Through a Recording

Press the round play button or the space bar to play and pause all stems together. Drag the scrub handle or click a waveform to move to a passage. The left and right arrow keys move five seconds at a time.

Choose 0.5×, 0.75×, or 1× to change the speed without changing the pitch. Slowing down can help you follow a phrase or hear a rhythm more clearly.

Solo and Mute

Choose Mute to silence one stem. Solo silences the others and changes the chosen stem's button to Only. You can solo several stems, such as vocals and drums, to listen to them together.

When you listen on your own, changes to the mix stay in your browser. During a live session, you can follow the instructor's playback while still using your own mute switches.

Repeat a Passage

Press L to loop the current region and listen again to a riff, cadence, or drum fill. Press Esc to clear both the loop and any solo.

Focus the mixer before using a shortcut. Keys control that recording without taking over text fields. The same legend appears below the notes in an expanded session.

When you choose two stems, instrumental contains the accompaniment. Instructors can rename labels in Class settings. See Class Workspace for course settings.

Listen to What Is There

The labels describe what the app tries to separate, but the results are not always exact. A stem can contain more than one instrument or include sounds from another part of the recording. Listen to each part and use the labels as a starting point for what you hear.
