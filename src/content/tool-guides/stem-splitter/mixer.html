Playback

In the Session Rack, you can open a recording to use its mixer. Press the round play button or Space to play and pause the selected stems. Drag the playback marker or click a waveform to move to a passage. ← and → move five seconds at a time.

Choose 0.5×, 0.75×, or 1× to change the speed without changing the pitch. Slowing down can make a phrase or rhythm easier to follow.

Solo and Mute

Choose Mute to silence one stem. Solo silences the others and changes the chosen stem's button to Only. You can solo several stems, such as vocals and drums, to listen to them together.

When you listen on your own, changes to the mix stay in your browser. During a live session, you can follow the instructor's playback while still using your own mute switches.

Loops

Press L to repeat the current passage. Press Esc to clear both the loop and any solo.

Shortcuts

KeyAction
SpacePlay or pause
← →Back or forward five seconds
1–9Mute or unmute stem 1–9, top to bottom
Shift+1–9Solo stem 1–9
[ ]Previous or next note
LSet a loop
EscClear the loop and any solo

Focus the mixer before using a shortcut. The shortcuts control that recording without interfering with text fields. You can also find them below the notes when a recording is open.

Labels and Colors

StemTypical contents
vocalsLead and backing voices. Spoken word and crowd noise may appear here too.
drumsDrum kit and most percussion.
bassElectric and upright bass, and low synth bass.
otherHorns, strings, organ, synths, and other sounds.
guitarAcoustic and electric guitar. Available when you choose six stems.
pianoPiano and some keyboard parts. Available when you choose six stems.

When you choose two stems, instrumental contains the accompaniment. Instructors can change labels in Class settings. See Class Workspace for course settings.

Reading the Labels

The labels describe what the app tries to separate, but the results are not always exact. A stem can contain more than one instrument or include sounds from another part of the recording. Listen to the stems to check what the app separated.
