Generative AI is changing higher education quickly, and many instructors are still working out what it means for their courses. In a new short video from the CUNY Graduate Center, Zach Muhlbauer, co-founder of the CUNY AI Lab (CAIL), talks about how faculty can meet this moment while keeping meaningful learning at the center of their teaching.
