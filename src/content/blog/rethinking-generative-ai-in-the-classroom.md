In this short video, CAIL co-founder Zach Muhlbauer offers recommendations for CUNY instructors interested in fostering critical and creative experimentation with generative AI. Zach discusses using—and breaking—small language models to demonstrate the fault lines of the technology, making models more interpretable and approachable through game-based learning, and building community through open and honest dialogue with students about the changing role of generative AI in both their learning and everyday lives.
