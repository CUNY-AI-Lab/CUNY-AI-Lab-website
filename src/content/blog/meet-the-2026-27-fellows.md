We're excited to welcome five graduate student fellows to the CUNY AI Lab (CAIL) for the 2026–27 academic year! Please join us in welcoming Kay Kim (M.S. in Data Analysis and Visualization), Ricardo Ledan (M.A. in Digital Humanities), Gabe Levine (Ph.D. in Computer Science), Sasha Richardson (M.A. in Digital Humanities), and Parisa M. Setayesh (Ph.D. in Earth and Environmental Sciences)!

Our [call for applications](/blog/cail-fellows-2026-27/) drew an enthusiastic response from master's and doctoral students across the Graduate Center, with many more strong applicants than we were able to support. This year's fellows bring a range of disciplinary perspectives and skills to the Lab.

Over the course of the academic year, they'll work alongside CAIL staff to test and improve our tools, support instructors and researchers using them, contribute to outreach and communications, and help us expand our community of practice across CUNY. They'll also share reflections on their work and the critical use of AI in teaching and research through contributions to this blog.

There will be opportunities to meet and work with our fellows throughout the year, starting with our two remaining [co-working sessions](/events/) this fall: November 17 and December 3, at the [New Media Lab](https://newmedialab.cuny.edu/) (Room 7388.01) at the Graduate Center. We encourage you to stop by, share what you're working on, and get feedback from our team. In the spring, the fellows will also help us organize and facilitate another round of workshops and co-working sessions.
