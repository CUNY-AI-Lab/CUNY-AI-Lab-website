Starting November 2, the CAIL Sandbox will use CUNY Single Sign-On (SSO) for all logins. Users will need an approved CUNY AI Lab account to access the Sandbox, and model usage will count toward their CUNY AI Lab quota.

As part of this transition, the previous email-and-password login option will be discontinued.

Existing Sandbox users were contacted with instructions for transferring their conversations and saved work to their CUNY SSO accounts. Accounts have been migrated for users who completed the migration form.

If you experience any issues logging in or accessing your previous work, please submit a support ticket through the [CAIL Help Desk](https://tools.ailab.gc.cuny.edu/help/).
