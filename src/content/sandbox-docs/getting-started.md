Start by choosing the access route that fits you. Students join through an instructor's invitation; instructors arranging a class apply for course access. An individual application is for your own use.

## Get Access<span id="request-access" aria-hidden="true"></span>

### Request Individual Access

1. Open the [individual access application](https://ailab.gc.cuny.edu/request-access/?kind=individual), select **My own access**, and sign in with **CUNY Login** if prompted.
2. Fill in your details and intended use. Tool interests are optional; one application covers the Lab's tools.
3. Complete the verification and select **Submit Application**. Watch your verified CUNY email for approval.

Individual access lets you use resources shared by the Lab. To create configurations in **Workspace**, request separate access from [ailab@gc.cuny.edu](mailto:ailab@gc.cuny.edu).

### Request Course Access

Get course approval before preparing the class environment or inviting students. You can apply without an existing individual Lab account.

1. Open the [course application](https://ailab.gc.cuny.edu/request-access/?kind=class), sign in with **CUNY Login**, and select **A class I'm teaching or organizing**.
2. Complete your details, planned use, and **Class details**, including dates and estimated enrollment. Check **I teach or lead this class**, complete the verification, and submit.
3. After approval by email, open the course in [My classes](https://tools.ailab.gc.cuny.edu/my-classes) and create or copy its invitation link.
4. Sign in to the [Sandbox](https://chat.ailab.gc.cuny.edu/) and confirm that its automatically created private group and text channel are available. Then share the invitation with students.

Continue to [Student Onboarding](student-onboarding.md#instructor-checkpoint-complete-course-access-first) before the first class activity.

### Join Course as a Student<span id="join-a-course-as-a-student" aria-hidden="true"></span>

Use your instructor's invitation link rather than submitting an individual access request. Open the invitation, sign in with **CUNY Login**, and confirm enrollment. If you need the link, ask your instructor. Enrollment and Sandbox sign-in add you to the course group and text channel automatically.

## First Login

With access approved or course enrollment confirmed, open the Sandbox in your browser:

1. Go to [chat.ailab.gc.cuny.edu](https://chat.ailab.gc.cuny.edu).
2. Select **Continue with CUNY Login**.
3. Enter your CUNY credentials and complete two-factor authentication if prompted.

You will arrive at the chat interface. Course participants should check that the course's private text channel is available. If login stalls, try a private browser window or another browser. Contact [ailab@gc.cuny.edu](mailto:ailab@gc.cuny.edu) if access or course resources are still missing.

## Your First Chat<span id="your-first-conversation" aria-hidden="true"></span>

Choose the model your instructor assigned, or try one available in the selector on the right inside the message box. Enter a short question about something you can check, then press Enter or use the send button. For example, paste a short public course passage and ask:

> Identify one claim in this passage and point to the wording that supports your reading.

Compare the answer with the passage. Does the cited wording support the claim? Ask a follow-up about anything unclear, and keep the source open while you check. [Sandbox Basics](sandbox-basics.md) continues this process.

To return later, use the three-dot menu beside the chat in the sidebar and choose **Rename**. Give it a title that identifies the task or reading.

## Adjust Your Settings

Open your profile menu and choose **Settings**. In **General**, you can change **Theme**; in **Interface**, **Enter to Send** controls sending and line breaks. **Account** contains your profile picture and display name. Click **Save**, then close Settings with **X** or Escape.

Leave personal **System Prompt** and **Advanced Parameters** at their defaults while getting oriented. To choose a default model, open a new chat, select a model, and choose **Set as default** in the selector.

## Privacy and Chat History

Requests to AI providers are configured for zero retention, and use of prompts and responses for model training is prohibited. Chats may still be saved in your Sandbox account, where administrators can access them. Sharing a chat or posting in a course channel makes that content available to its audience. Do not upload personal, confidential, or other people's work without appropriate permission. See the [Lab's Sandbox description](https://ailab.gc.cuny.edu/tools/).
